# EvidenceDesk workflow ad

This folder contains the source material for the launch video.

- `evidencedesk-hero.png` is AI-generated abstract background art. It is used as atmosphere, not as evidence of a product feature.
- The product shots are real captures of the local EvidenceDesk application.
- `FLOW_3_ACCOUNT_PRODUCTION.md` is the harmonized three-account Google Flow plan.
- `narration-v2.txt` is the new 60-second ElevenLabs script. Flow should not generate speech.
- `captions-v2.srt` is the authoritative subtitle track.
- `evidencedesk-logo-reference.png` is the shared visual anchor for all three Flow projects.
- `evidencedesk-about-light.png`, `evidencedesk-workspace-light.png`, `evidencedesk-library-light.png`, and `evidencedesk-cited-answer-light.png` are real 1920×1080 captures of the redesigned application.
- `northstar-employee-handbook-demo.txt` is the synthetic policy used for the cited-answer capture.

The earlier local FFmpeg build remains in this folder. The v2 production plan uses three Flow projects for visuals, music, and effects; ElevenLabs supplies narration during final assembly.

Image-generation prompt: “Premium cinematic 16:9 abstract technology background for EvidenceDesk, document planes flowing through a verification portal into highlighted evidence, near-black and forest green with mint, cyan, violet and restrained gold light, no text, no logos, no people, no robots.”

Honest scope: EvidenceDesk is presented as a local portfolio MVP with a standard AWS deployment path. The video makes no GovCloud, certification, enterprise-scale, or production-operations claim.
