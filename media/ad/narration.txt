Policy answers should not depend on guesswork.

Meet EvidenceDesk: a secure, citation-first assistant for organizational documents.

Upload a handbook, policy, procedure, or scanned PDF. EvidenceDesk extracts the text, uses OCR when needed, and stores useful metadata.

Before search begins, it checks the user’s tenant, role, and clearance. Restricted text is filtered before it can reach the answer workflow.

Then a controlled pipeline retrieves relevant passages, expands nearby context, ranks the evidence, writes a grounded answer, and verifies its citations.

The result is not just a confident paragraph. It is an answer you can inspect, with exact source passages. If the documents do not support an answer, EvidenceDesk refuses instead of pretending.

Conversations, feedback, document versions, audit events, and retrieval insights make the experience practical—not just a chatbot demo.

This is an honest local portfolio MVP, with automated evaluation, Docker, continuous integration, and a clear path to standard AWS services.

EvidenceDesk. Evidence before confidence.
