AI can give a confident answer in seconds. But when the question is about policy, procedure, or compliance, confidence is not proof.

EvidenceDesk changes the workflow. It answers from the organization’s own approved documents and shows where every supported claim came from.

Upload a searchable or scanned PDF. EvidenceDesk validates it, applies OCR when needed, and turns it into retrievable evidence. Ask a normal question. It finds the relevant passages first, then writes a grounded answer. Open any citation to inspect the exact page and highlighted source.

Access is scoped before retrieval, and weak evidence triggers a clear refusal instead of a guess. That makes EvidenceDesk useful for handbooks, procedures, operational knowledge, and document review.
