Annual Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 20 working days of paid annual leave in each calendar year. Employees should submit ordinary leave requests at least 10 working days before the intended start date. A line manager may approve or decline a request after considering team coverage and operational needs.

Up to 5 unused annual-leave days may be carried into the following calendar year. Carried days must be used by 31 March or they expire. Annual leave is separate from sick leave and public holidays.
