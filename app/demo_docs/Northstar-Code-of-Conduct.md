# Northstar Labs Code of Conduct

> Fictional document created solely for the EvidenceDesk portfolio demonstration.

## Respectful workplace

Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and partners with dignity and respect. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, and retaliation are prohibited.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or family interest that could influence a business decision. Disclosures should be submitted to the Compliance Officer before the employee participates in the affected decision.

## Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept modest promotional items, but they must not offer or accept cash, expensive gifts, or hospitality intended to influence a business decision.

## Reporting misconduct

Employees should report suspected misconduct to their manager, People Operations, or the confidential ethics hotline at ethics@example.test. Reports may be made anonymously. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.

## Confidential information

Employees must protect confidential company, customer, and employee information. Confidential information may be shared only with authorized people who have a legitimate business need.

## Violations

Confirmed violations may result in corrective action, including training, a written warning, suspension, or termination, depending on the circumstances.
