# Northstar Labs Employee Handbook

Synthetic demonstration document — no real employee or company information.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave in each calendar year. Leave accrues monthly from the employee's start date. Employees should submit planned leave through the company leave system at least 10 working days before the first day of absence. The employee's manager approves requests based on team coverage. Up to five unused annual-leave days may be carried into the next calendar year; any additional unused days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days per calendar year. A medical certificate is required after three consecutive working days of absence. Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

## Parental leave

Eligible primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Eligible secondary caregivers receive four weeks. Employees should contact People Operations at least eight weeks before the expected start date when possible.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Roles involving on-site equipment or regulated facilities may have different requirements.

## Reporting concerns

Employees may report suspected misconduct to their manager, People Operations, or the confidential ethics channel. Retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern is prohibited.
